About Frontier Forestry

Honest deals and real connections — that's what this business is built on.

I'm Caleb Happy, owner of Frontier Forestry Equipment. I got my start in the forestry equipment world back in 2021, cutting my teeth in marketing and sales at a major equipment dealership. Over the years I worked my way through every corner of the business — selling tracks, tires, attachments, and eventually whole machines when the opportunity came along.

That hands-on experience taught me how the industry really works, from the ground up. I learned what matters to the people who run this equipment every day, and I built my reputation on being straightforward, professional, and easy to work with.

Frontier Forestry Equipment is the result of all that experience. I'm here to connect buyers and sellers without overcomplicating things — no pressure, no runaround. Just good equipment, fair deals, and someone who actually picks up the phone.

What We Do

Full-service forestry equipment brokerage — from finding the right machine to closing the deal.

Buy & Sell Equipment

Looking to buy or sell logging and firewood equipment? I'll match you with the right buyer or machine, handle the details, and keep the process simple from start to finish.

Equipment Appraisals

Need to know what your equipment is worth? I provide honest, market-informed valuations based on real-world experience — not just book values.

Consignment Sales

Don't have time to sell it yourself? I'll list your equipment, market it to the right buyers, and handle the sale — you just collect the check.
